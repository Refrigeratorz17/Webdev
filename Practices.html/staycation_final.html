Bear Lake!

Bear Lake is a large freshwater lake. It's known for it's bright turquoise water and it is called the "Caribbean of the Rockies". It is a popular destination for those looking for a fun summer vacation and even a winter one!

Where it's located!

Border of Utah and Idaho, but this website is about the side in Utah. It specifically is on the border of Rich county, Utah, which is situated in the northeastern part of Utah. Is a significant geological feature situated in a fault-bounced valley. The Utah side is apart of Bear Lake State Park
